Most modern security classifiers rely on superficial feature correlation, leading to massive false alarm rates.

## Beyond Correlation

By applying causal graphing and structural equation modeling, we isolate robust protocol invariants that represent structural spec violations.

### The Causal Framework

- Build directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) of protocol dependencies
- Use **do-calculus** to distinguish causation from confounding
- Identify invariant features that hold across network conditions
